How each number is calculated

Accuracy notes. Prices are mid = (bid + ask) / 2. The move between the last tick of one candle and the first tick of the next is counted in the new candle, so 5m/1h/4h candles are exact sums of 1m candles. Runs and reversals across minute boundaries are very close approximations. History downloaded from OANDA has prices only (metrics are empty). Volume is tick count, not traded volume: forex has no central tape, so direction is inferred from quote changes.
